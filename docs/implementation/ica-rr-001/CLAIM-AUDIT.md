## Existing limitations, not silently repaired

- Native waist dispositions retain distinct HOLD/DENY values but share `RIO_DENIED_OR_HELD` as a reason for these two policy fixtures. The fixture declaration identifies the actual configured policy; richer native denial explanation is not supplied. This lossy reason coordinate is returned as evidence, not repaired by changing the frozen owner.
- The existing native Return compresses readback into OBSERVED/FAILED and does not carry an independent objective assessment. The completed consequence specimen preserves separate qualified Evidence, objective and reporting completeness beside it. This ICA surface uses that account and does not relabel the native legacy field as objective success.
- The earlier hardening report identifies legacy gateway kill/termination wording stronger than its recorded controls. ICA does not invoke that endpoint. See `docs/implementation/consequence-return-hardening/EXISTING-CLAIM-AUDIT.md`; this build does not repair that broader runtime.
- The local controller capability qualifies a reference session, not production human identity, distributed revocation or proof of physical irreversibility.
- The register supports live-session reentry and exported inspection, not cross-host/cross-process capability restoration. Serialized artifacts gain no standing by successful transport or rendering.

No constitutional contradiction was repaired or new doctrine introduced. Permanent pressure-differential morphology remains open exactly as reported by the radar implementation.
