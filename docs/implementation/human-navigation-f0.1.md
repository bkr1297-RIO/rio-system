# Human Navigation Runtime F0.1

Candidate implementation below the Conformance Cut, inside the existing ICA orientation path. This component does not amend ONE, add an Office or source domain, or replace a constitutional owner. The conceptual frontier remains #212. The sealed F1 Seed and its exact predecessor remain unchanged.

The governing implementation contract is: expand navigational resolution without occupying human authorship. ENOUGH_C judges the Counterpart's presently available contribution, never human sufficiency.

## Operations and controller

| Operation | Machine contract |
|---|---|
| BEHOLD(source) | Copies an attributable source into an immutable view, retaining its semantic type, standing, content and basis. |
| ORIENT(movement, context) | Organizes relevant sources, relations, constraints, open questions, possibilities and prior Returns. It creates no selected direction or permission. |
| ENOUGH_C(context, movement, candidates) | True when no declared, supported, relevant, undelivered material contribution remains for this movement. |
| RETURN_CHOICE(movement, context) | Returns an unresolved human valuation with its basis and human authorship; selects no option. |
| YIELD(movement, context, candidates) | Ends foreground contribution when its burden is exhausted and neither relevant HOLD nor human valuation remains. |

The controller prioritizes a missing current operating envelope or materially necessary relevant dependency, then pending human valuation, then supported material additions. Otherwise it yields.

| Typed outcome | Meaning | Consequential effect |
|---|---|---|
| FLOW | Continue descriptive contribution within the existing orientation envelope. | None |
| HOLD | A necessary, relevant condition or current envelope is unavailable. | None; this is not a new ConstitutionalDisposition |
| RETURN_CHOICE | Legitimate human valuation remains. | None; this does not author a HumanChoice |
| YIELD | No additional material Counterpart contribution is presently warranted. | None; this says nothing about human understanding or objective completion |

Five separate material burdens can support contribution: new information, new distinction, relevant contradiction, changed dependency, or newly relevant risk. Each candidate also carries `human_choice_pending` and `requested_continuation`; those two flags alone cannot establish materially additive contribution. A request to continue is not permission to manufacture another recommendation.

Every candidate binds the movement, relevant source, source basis and contribution identity. Unrelated or already delivered contributions cannot occupy the foreground. Undeclared directive fields, unknown references, accessor-backed input and unsupported provenance are rejected. Outcome objects are immutable and owner-issued; a JSON copy cannot be presented to the renderer as an issued outcome.

## Existing-room lowering

The adapter consumes the existing issued ICA account. It uses the current FieldoscopyReading, native relevant HOLD, Research Return, explicit retention and separate account admission. There is no new command, endpoint, model, Office, sensor, scheduler or notification loop.

An exact reading is delivered when its own INSPECT and EXPLAIN register entries exist. This is delivery of the Counterpart contribution, not a conclusion that the human understands enough. A later reading has a new identity and can reopen descriptive FLOW; old inspection flags cannot consume it. Earlier interpretations remain in lineage.

A Research Return can offer the human a carrying choice while knowledge and outcome remain unresolved. The existing `keep` choice or separate `admit-account` choice answers that carrying burden. Reporting acceptance alone does not answer it. Neither retention nor account admission produces a new Research permission.

Authenticated historical re-entry is read-only orientation. It yields without reviving an operative envelope, lease, delegation or current carrying question. UNKNOWN, obligations and typed Perimeter residue remain visible.

The existing observational paragraph renders the current navigation foreground. YIELD renders the empty string: no question, suggestion, related topic, or engagement tail. Existing pull controls, evidence, eight lived answers and Perimeter remain available. An empty contribution is not a blank room or removal of human control.
